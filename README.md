# Task 2: Data Visualization and Storytelling

## Project Overview

This project was completed as part of my internship Task 2: Data Visualization and Storytelling.

The objective is to transform sales data into meaningful visualizations that communicate a clear and compelling business story.

## Objective

To analyze sales data and create visualizations that help identify:

- Sales trends over time
- Best-performing product categories
- Regional sales performance
- Customer segment performance
- Key business opportunities

## Tools Used

- Tableau / Power BI
- Microsoft Excel
- Data Visualization
- Data Storytelling

## Dataset

The project uses a Superstore sales dataset containing information about orders, customers, products, categories, regions and sales.

## Visualizations Created

- KPI Overview
- Year-wise Sales Trend
- Sales by Category
- Regional Sales Performance
- Customer Segment Analysis
- Top Sub-Category Analysis
- Top Product Analysis

## Key Business Insights

- Total sales are approximately **$2.26 million**.
- The dataset contains **4,922 unique orders**.
- There are approximately **793 unique customers**.
- **Technology** is the highest-performing category with approximately **$827K** in sales.
- The **West** region is the strongest region with approximately **$710K** in sales.
- Sales show a strong upward trend toward 2018.

## Business Story

The visualization shows that overall sales performance has improved over time, with Technology emerging as the strongest product category and the West region contributing the highest regional sales.

These insights can help businesses focus on high-performing categories and regions while identifying opportunities to improve weaker areas.

## Business Recommendations

- Continue investing in high-performing product categories.
- Identify strategies to improve sales in weaker regions.
- Monitor sales trends regularly.
- Use customer segment performance to create targeted marketing strategies.
- Analyze top-performing products to support inventory and sales planning.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates how raw sales data can be transformed into clear visual insights and a compelling business story using data visualization techniques.

The dashboard focuses not only on presenting charts, but also on explaining what happened, where it happened, what contributed to the results, and what the business can do next.
